## The math (recomputed — do NOT trust the numbers in the brief)

$500/month, 40 years, monthly compounding, ordinary annuity (deposit at month end):

| | net return | final pot |
|---|---|---|
| no extra fee | 7.0% / yr | **$1,312,407** |
| 1% higher fee | 6.0% / yr | **$995,745** |
| | **gap** | **$316,661** |

- Gap as a share of the bigger pot: **316,661 / 1,312,407 = 24.13%** → "a quarter". ✅
- Total actually contributed: $240,000 — so the gap alone is **bigger than every dollar he deposited over 40 years combined**. (Not narrated; it's the follow-up video.)
- Hasan's brief said ~$1.31M / ~$996k / ~$317k / ~24%. **All four confirmed.** Title keeps "a quarter", and the on-screen twist number is the exact 24%.
- The curves in the TSX are generated by `contribSeries()` at render time from the same
  formula, so the drawn line and the counted number cannot disagree.

## The retime (the lesson this short paid for)

The pre-voice estimates grew the curves slowly across the whole reveal (17.4→26.6). The real word
times exposed that as broken: he *names both pot values at 21.3s*, when the curves would still have
been mid-growth and the head labels would have read **~$1.0M / ~$780K** — the wrong numbers, under
the words that say the right ones.

Fix: **split one progress into two cues.** Curve growth and the gap counter are now independent —
the curves land under the words that name the pots (21.3), and the counter runs under the words that
name the gap (26.0→28.9). Generalized rule for the niche: *one animated quantity per spoken number,
each landing on its own word.*

## Production notes

- **Frame-0 rule.** BigTitle is `warm`, the ticker/end-dots/curves are all driven by an
  explicit `grow` value that is **1.0 at frame 0** (not ramped from a component's own `at`).
  The rewind is what animates, not the appearance.
- **The rewind is the setup** (the "undo the canvas" retention device from IDEAS.md). It also
  earns the loop for free: the reveal simply re-draws to the state frame 0 was already in.
- **Loop closes:** at f=1260 the curves are full, the gap is shaded, the ticker reads the final
  number, the ShareBar is gone and the title is back — pixel-for-pixel the hook.
- **No outro CTA** (locked 2026-07-12). Ends on the payoff line; the loop is the ending.
- Sequence-local frames: every cue in the TSX is computed on the GLOBAL frame (the shot has no
  nested Sequences except the PauseCard), so `local_f = global_f - from` only applies there.
- Colors: gold `#f5d76e` = the honest 7% investor; red `#ef5f6b` = the fee and everything it eats.
